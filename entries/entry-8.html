Keki Modern Cakes is a popular spot in NYC’s Chinatown known for its light, fluffy, and super bouncy cheesecakes. Their signature cakes are soft and airy, made with high-quality ingredients. Now operating as Let’s Bounce, the bakery continues to offer cheesecakes and other baked goods like sponge cakes and cream puffs.
