Welcome to Molly Tea!

Molly Tea is a modern tea‑house brand originally from Shenzhen, China, that specializes in jasmine‑scented teas crafted with a contemporary “Eastern Modern” aesthetic. Their menu features floral‑infused milk tea drinks, oat milk teas, and signature “snowy whipped cream” teas, all designed to deliver a rich floral aroma and delicate taste, blending traditional Chinese tea heritage with modern style and sensibility.
