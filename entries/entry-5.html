Ming Wong is a long-standing Chinese restaurant serving traditional Cantonese dishes. It’s known for classic items like roast duck, BBQ pork, and noodle and rice plates that are popular for both dine-in and takeout. The restaurant has a casual, straightforward setting and is known for its solid portions.

If you’re exploring Mott Street, don’t miss stopping by Ming Wong for a hearty, satisfying meal!
